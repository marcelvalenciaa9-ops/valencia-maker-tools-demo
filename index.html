Questions before you buy

Will it connect to Etsy, Shopify or my payment processor?

No. You enter your own fees and costs. The file does not sync to an ecommerce platform.

Does it calculate income tax or VAT?

No. The modeled profit is based on entered item costs and selling fees; it does not calculate income taxes, sales tax or actual future earnings.

Can I open it on my phone?

The workbook is designed primarily for desktop spreadsheet use. The free demo on this page is mobile-friendly. We have not verified every mobile spreadsheet application.

Will this demo store my numbers?

No. This page sends no values to a server. The optional CSV export saves a local copy in your browser downloads; you remain responsible for storing it.
